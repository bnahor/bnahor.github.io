Most alerting stacks fail for a simple reason: they optimize for _detection_ but not for _resolution_.

## The practical shift

We moved from dashboard-first triage to owner-first triage:

- Every alert maps to an owner and an escalation path.
- Every alert includes a recovery playbook.
- Every alert is tuned against actionability, not raw sensitivity.

```ts
const shouldPage = (signalScore: number, ownerAvailable: boolean) => {
  return signalScore >= 0.85 && ownerAvailable;
};
```

The result was fewer noisy notifications and faster recovery loops.

## What I keep doing

I now treat incident ergonomics as a product surface, not an ops afterthought.
